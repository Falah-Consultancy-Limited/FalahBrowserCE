Falah Browser is not just a tool; it is a **Lantern**. Unlike traditional browsers that prioritize data extraction, Falah prioritizes your **Heart (Qalb)**. It integrates real-time Shariah guidance, prayer awareness, and spiritual reflection directly into your browsing workflow.

<a name="installation-guide"></a>
## 📥 2. Installation Guide

### macOS (Recommended)
1. Download the latest `FalahBrowser-1.0.0.dmg`.
2. Open the disk image and drag **Falah Browser** to your `Applications` folder.
3. **Note**: On first launch, you may need to right-click and select "Open" to bypass unidentified developer warnings.

### Windows
1. Run `FalahBrowser-Setup-1.0.0.exe`.
2. Follow the installation wizard.
3. A shortcut will be created on your Desktop and Start Menu.

### Linux (Debian/Ubuntu)
```bash
sudo dpkg -i falah-browser_1.0.0_amd64.deb
sudo apt-get install -f
```

<a name="initial-setup"></a>
## ⚙️ 3. Initial Setup

### Step 1: Connect your Ummah ID
Upon first launch, you will be prompted to link your **Ummah ID**. This ensures that your spiritual goals and prayer preferences stay synchronized across the Falah OS ecosystem without ever touching a centralized cloud.

### Step 2: Set your Spiritual Compass
1. Click the **Lantern Icon** in the top right corner.
2. Select **Settings**.
3. Under **Location**, enter your City and Country. This is critical for accurate **Prayer Times** and **Qibla** detection.

### Step 3: Choose your Guidance Level
Falah offers three modes of Shariah Guidance:
*   **Gentle (Default)**: Visual "Whispers" and ethical alternatives.
*   **Strict**: Automatic blocking of sites flagged for severe Shariah non-compliance.
*   **Observer**: Silent logging of habits for your weekly **Reflection Mode** report.

<a name="spiritual-ecosystem"></a>
## 🕌 4. The Spiritual Ecosystem

### 🏮 Lantern Home (New Tab)
Your default home page features:
*   **Daily Ayah/Hadith**: A curated verse to inspire your day.
*   **Sunnah Checklist**: Quick-tap items for Dhikr, Quran, and Sadaqah.
*   **Prayer Countdown**: A live timer showing exactly how long until the next Salat.

### 🧘 Reflection Mode
Access this via `falah://reflection`. Every Friday (or upon your request), Falah presents a "Mirror" of your digital soul—showing where you spent your time and suggesting ways to purify your habits.

<a name="ai-guidance"></a>
## 🛡️ 5. AI Shariah Guidance (The Whisper)

As you browse, the **Falah AI Sidecar** analyzes page content in real-time.

*   **Verdicts**:
    *   🟢 **Safe**: Content aligns with Islamic values.
    *   🟡 **Caution**: Potential Riba (interest), intoxicants, or ethically grey content detected.
    *   🔴 **Blocked**: Prohibited content (Gambling, etc.) is intercepted.
*   **The Whisper Panel**: When a site is flagged as "Caution," a gold-tinted panel slides out from the right, providing a gentle reminder and suggesting **Halal Alternatives**.

<a name="security"></a>
## 🔐 6. Data Sovereignty
Your data is **Amanah** (a trust). 
*   **Zero-Cloud Sync**: Your history, bookmarks, and passwords never leave your machine.
*   **Local Encryption**: All data is stored in an encrypted database using **AES-256**, with keys stored in your system's secure keychain.
