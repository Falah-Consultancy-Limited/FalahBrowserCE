The Halal Token Engine

The world's first blockchain-native halal certification system. Every facility, product, ingredient and logistics chain carries an immutable on-chain token — scannable, verifiable, cross-border. Like a halal stamp that can never be forged, bought, or expired without the certifying body's cryptographic signature.

Halal Certificate Token

Non-transferable soulbound token representing halal certification for facilities, products, processes, and ingredients. Issued by accredited bodies, expires and renews on-chain.
